# Features

> Last updated: 2026-05-06  
> Version: 1.0.0

## Implemented Features
- **Dashboard & Analytics:** Real-time metrics overview (audits in progress, open recommendations, high risks).
- **Internal Audit Management Lifecycle:** End-to-end management spanning Audit Programs, Plans, Tasks (Fieldwork), Findings, Evidence gathering, and Recommendations tracking.
- **Official Correspondence System (CMS):** Complete automation of incoming and outgoing mail, including secure attachments, referral tracking, and intelligent serial numbering.
- **Risk & Compliance Registers:** Tools to maintain the enterprise Risk Register, Central Bank Instructions, and an internal Law Bank.
- **Integrity & Fraud Management:** Highly confidential modules for handling Whistleblowing, Fraud Logs, and Conflict of Interest disclosures.
- **Organizational Structure Modeling:** Tree-based visualizer and management system for departments, job titles, and reporting lines.
- **Extensive Security Audit Trail:** An immutable "black box" that silently records every creation, modification, or deletion action taken by any user.
- **Deep-File Security Validation:** All uploaded attachments are scanned using AI (Google Magika) to detect MIME-type spoofing or hidden malware signatures before saving to disk.
- **Multi-language Support:** Complete RTL (Arabic) and LTR (English) interfaces.
- **Custom Report Generation:** PDF and DOCX exporting capabilities for findings and audit reports.

## Features in Development
- **Advanced Permissions Granularity:** Refining exact row-level scopes (e.g., restricted visibility for specific findings based on dynamic matrices).
- **Full-Text PDF Search:** Improving the ability to search within the contents of locally stored PDF evidence and laws.

## Planned Features
- **Advanced BI Dashboards:** Custom user-defined widgets for deeper data visualization (D3/Recharts extensions).
- **Automated Email Integration:** Linking the internal notification system to strict internal SMTP servers for off-platform alerts.
