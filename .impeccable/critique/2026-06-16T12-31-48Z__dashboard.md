## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The two primary AI-product reflexes called out in the first critique have both been resolved. The hero-metric template (12 equal-weight KPI cards) is gone — replaced by a genuine priority/secondary split with collapsible groups. Uppercase `tracking-widest` has been stripped from all non-badge locations; card titles, action labels, and metadata now use regular sentence-case type at appropriate sizes. The dashboard no longer reads as generated product UI at a glance.

Remaining minor signal: the Alerts card and Quick Actions card are still visually similar glass-cards stacked in the same column. The differentiation (all-clear state on Alerts, action-buttons on Quick Actions) is functional, but the two cards share enough visual vocabulary that the column can feel repetitive on a clean system state. Not a hard slop tell — more a rhythm issue.

**Deterministic scan**: Detector unavailable (`impeccable-config.mjs` missing). All findings from manual review.

**Visual overlays**: Not attempted — no dev server running.

## Overall Impression

This is a meaningfully different dashboard from the original critique. The information architecture is now defensible: the four most urgent metrics lead, secondary detail groups follow and collapse. Tooltips give every metric a plain-language explanation. The filter-change feedback loop is tight. The alerts card is honest about system state. The typography hierarchy is clean. The score jump from 24 → 32 reflects real work, not cosmetic fixes.

The remaining headroom is almost entirely in Flexibility & Efficiency (power user features) and in minor polish: the column rhythm of Quick Actions + Alerts, a few missing tooltips on group labels, and the absence of date-range scoping on the filter. None of those are blocking; they're the difference between "good" and "excellent."

## What's Working

1. **Information architecture**: The 4-card priority row + 3 collapsible secondary groups is the right structure for an audit dashboard. Critical metrics lead; secondary detail follows and can be hidden. Working-memory load is now within bounds.
2. **Contextual help coverage**: Tooltips on every KPI title and every filter tab is a complete implementation of Recognition over Recall. The bar chart legend and risk legend are visible without hover — correct for a compliance surface where users may be printing or using screen readers.
3. **Conditional alert state**: The Alerts card truthfully reflects system state. The all-clear (`BellOff` in success green) is a considered design decision — it's reassuring, not neutral. The conditional border and pulse animation are directly tied to data.

## Priority Issues

**[P2] Quick Actions + Alerts column — two similarly-weighted glass-cards, no visual differentiation**
- **What**: Both cards in the right column use `glass-card p-6` with `text-base font-bold` headers and identical spacing. On a clean system state (all-clear), the column presents two content areas of nearly equal visual weight.
- **Why it matters**: The column reads as two separate sections that happen to be stacked, rather than one coherent sidebar. On a clean day, the Alerts card is mostly empty green; the Quick Actions card is the primary value. The visual hierarchy doesn't reflect that.
- **Fix**: Merge the two cards into one panel: Quick Actions above, a thin divider, Alerts status below. Or give Alerts card a compact summary mode (just the bell icon + "System clear" in one line) when `isAllClear`, expanding to full view when there are active alerts. This would let the Quick Actions card breathe.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout Dashboard quick-actions column`

**[P2] Collapsible group labels have no tooltip explaining what they cover**
- **What**: Group labels "Audits & Recommendations", "Risks", "Correspondence & Compliance" are plain text buttons — no tooltip explaining that clicking collapses the group, and no description of what each group contains vs. the highlight row.
- **Why it matters**: A new compliance officer opening the dashboard will wonder why some metrics are in the top row and others in collapsible groups. The distinction (critical/urgent vs. operational/monitoring) is not communicated.
- **Fix**: Add a `title` or Tooltip to each group label button: e.g. "Click to collapse. These are secondary monitoring metrics." Or add a brief sub-label under the group heading: "Monitoring metrics" vs. "Alert metrics."
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify Dashboard KPI groups`

**[P3] Filter scope ambiguity — do tabs filter charts and activity feed, or only KPIs?**
- **What**: Clicking "Operational" updates KPI counts via `useDashboardStats(activeFilter)`, but the charts (audit progress bar chart, risk pie) and activity feed may or may not respond depending on what the hook returns. No visual affordance distinguishes "this section filters" from "this section is always global."
- **Why it matters**: If charts and activity feed stay global while KPIs filter, users will be confused when numbers don't reconcile. If they do all filter, the loading overlay should cover the full page, not just the KPI grid.
- **Fix**: Expand the opacity overlay to cover all data sections during filter refetch (not just the KPI grid). Or add a "scoped to: Operational" badge near sections that are affected.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden Dashboard filter scope`

## Persona Red Flags

**Audit Manager (Power User)**: The priority row immediately surfaces high-risk findings and overdue recommendations — exactly what they need to triage in the morning. Collapsible secondary groups let them hide correspondence noise. Keyboard navigation on cards works. Remaining friction: no shortcut to jump to the highest-urgency item, no ability to pin or reorder the highlight row, no export of the current dashboard view.

**New Compliance Officer (First-Timer)**: Tooltip on "High-Risk Findings" now explains "Open findings rated High or Critical severity" — this directly resolves the original confusion about what that metric means versus "Open Findings." The filter tabs now have scope descriptions on hover. The activity feed has an empty state with a message. This persona is substantially better served than before.

**RTL Arabic User (Primary Audience)**: Wide letter-spacing is gone from all Arabic text labels. The tooltip CSS uses `inset-inline-start` for RTL-aware positioning. Progress bar, chart axes, and slide-in animations are direction-aware. No new RTL issues introduced.

## Minor Observations

- The `Tooltip` component's CSS uses `translateX(-50%)` for centering — works in LTR but the RTL override switches to `translateX(50%)`. This is correct in principle but the centering math depends on the tooltip always being narrower than the trigger element. Long tooltip text (e.g. Arabic descriptions) may clip or overflow. Worth testing with Arabic strings.
- `DashboardActivityFeed` now has a proper empty state with `empty-state-title` — good. The `empty-state-icon` uses `History` which fits the context well.
- The `isFirstLoad` ref pattern is clean — avoids showing the skeleton on filter-change refetches. However if the component unmounts and remounts (e.g. route change), `isFirstLoad.current` resets to `true` and the skeleton will show again, which is actually the correct behaviour.
- `DashboardRiskOverview` is now properly typed (`RiskLevel`, `RiskStats`, `DashboardStats`). The `t` prop type `(key: string, ...args: unknown[]) => string` is more accurate than `any`. Remaining: `DashboardAuditProgress`, `DashboardActivityFeed`, and `DashboardQuickActions` still accept `t: any` — these could be tightened to the same signature.
- The Tooltip component's `role="presentation"` on the wrapper span is correct for decorative containers. However, the `title` attribute on the outer span creates a native browser tooltip that may appear *in addition to* the CSS `::after` tooltip on some browsers (especially Firefox). Consider using `aria-describedby` on the inner element pointing to a visually-hidden description instead, to avoid duplicate tooltip triggers.

## Questions to Consider

- "The Quick Actions + Alerts column: should they be one unified panel that changes mode, or two cards? The all-clear state suggests they could be merged into a status-and-actions panel."
- "The filter tabs — do they scope all sections or just the KPI grid? If only the KPIs, should the charts be filterable too, or is 'global view' the right default for charts?"
- "Collapsible groups default to open. Should they remember their state across page navigations (localStorage)? For power users who always collapse Correspondence, that would be a meaningful efficiency gain."
